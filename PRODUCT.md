# Product

## Users

Friend groups listening together in Discord voice channels. They want to start music quickly, add tracks conversationally, understand what is playing, and share control without learning a command manual or configuring the bot first.

## Product Purpose

Vybatron turns a Discord voice channel into an easy shared listening space. Success means a friend can play a track, understand the queue, and use the common controls on the first try while the bot stays reliable and unobtrusive in the channel.

## Brand Personality

Confident, musical, and welcoming, with subtle Transformers-inspired energy. The name combines vibes with Cybertron; that identity should appear through concise language and a coherent visual accent without overpowering the music or becoming novelty role-play.

## Anti-references

Feature-heavy music bots that feel like command manuals: emoji clutter, novelty copy, crowded controls, configuration before playback, noisy channel spam, and Transformers references that become gimmicky. Avoid interfaces that rely on color alone or require friends to understand implementation details.

## Design Principles

- Make the first song effortless: the shortest path from intent to audible playback wins.
- Put the music first: artwork, track identity, playback state, and the next useful action outrank bot branding.
- Keep shared control obvious: use familiar Discord affordances, clear labels, and immediate feedback.
- Express Vybatron with restraint: a distinct voice and visual signature should flavor the experience without adding friction.
- Earn trust quietly: predictable behavior, graceful recovery, and low channel noise are part of the premium experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Accessibility is the default. Use clear text labels, color-independent status, concise layouts that work well on mobile Discord, low cognitive load, and plain-language recovery guidance. Avoid unnecessary animation and visual noise; common actions should remain understandable without prior bot knowledge.
